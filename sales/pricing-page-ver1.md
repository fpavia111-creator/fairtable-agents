# Fairtable pricing (ver 1)

The software is free (MIT). You pay a fixed price for the setup: we install the agents on your computer, map your exports (Toast, Square, Clover, Lightspeed, Revel, 7shifts, Homebase, MarginEdge, Restaurant365, xtraCHEF, Sysco and US Foods invoices, DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub statements, OpenTable or Resy), set your targets and your state's rules, run the first reports with you and train one person on your team.

Prices are set below the medical families (Fairclaim, Fairpractice) because independent restaurants and small groups run on thinner margins and smaller back offices. Kurt approved using these proposals.

## Support after setup

- $150 a month for one pack
- $300 a month for the whole restaurant
- $200 a month per location for groups and partner rollouts

Support covers changes in your exports (a POS or distributor changing its columns), rule and rate updates you ask for (a new minimum wage, a new tax rate), new versions of the agents and a monthly check-in call.

## Included and not included

Included: installation on your computer, mapping your exports, your settings (targets, wage and tip rules, tax rates, promo list, inspection intervals, allergen list), the first run of every agent in the package with you, one training session, the first reports.

Not included: paying, refunding, charging cards, changing your POS, publishing schedules, running payroll, filing taxes or messaging anyone (the agents never do these; a person on your team does). Legal, tax, payroll, health-code or food-safety advice. Logging in to your POS, delivery apps, distributors or bank.

Your files and reports stay on your computer. Staff and guest names are masked in anything the agents send to an AI model.
