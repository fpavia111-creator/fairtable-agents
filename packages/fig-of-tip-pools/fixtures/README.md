# Fixtures

Two fictional weeks; names are made up and the wage settings are examples, not any
state's current law. Planted: a manager whose card tips were not pooled
(manager_kept_tips), a host paid under the tipped cash wage (below_cash_wage), two servers
whose slow second week leaves cash wage plus tips under the minimum (tip_credit_shortfall),
and a server with card tips and no cash tips declared (no_cash_declared).
