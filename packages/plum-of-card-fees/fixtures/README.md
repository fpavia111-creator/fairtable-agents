# Fixtures

Fictional card statements for two fictional locations; the agreed rates are example
settings. Planted: October markup at Main Street over the agreed 0.30% + 0.10
(markup_over_contract), the effective rate up about 0.5 points (effective_rate_up), a
monthly fee over its cap (fee_over_cap), a PCI non-compliance fee (unexpected_fee) and a
non-qualified surcharge (downgrade_surcharge).
