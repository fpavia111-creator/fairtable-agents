# Fixtures

A fictional kitchen week; names are made up and the rules are example settings. Planted:
a line cook at 48 hours (weekly_ot), a prep cook with a split shift spread over 14 hours
(split_shift, spread_of_hours), a dishwasher who closed at 01:00 and opened at 07:00
(clopen) and has a shift with no clock-out (missing_clock_out), and a sous chef at four
10-hour days (daily_ot only under daily rules).
