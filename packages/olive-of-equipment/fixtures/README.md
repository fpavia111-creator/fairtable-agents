# Fixtures

A fictional equipment list and service log. The intervals are example settings, not a
fire code or a manufacturer's schedule. Planted: the hood cleaning and the fire
suppression service overdue (overdue), the grease trap and the extinguishers due within
30 days (due_soon), a freezer with no service date (no_last_service), a dish machine with
no interval (no_interval), and a walk-in repaired three times in two months
(repeat_repairs).
